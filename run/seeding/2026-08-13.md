## ch1 Owera Software (EN)

### 1. r/LocalLLaMA — comment on a fresh VRAM-spill / vLLM vs llama.cpp thread
**Draft:**
If the model fits, vLLM’s batching wins. If it *doesn’t*, I’ve seen vLLM OOM at the ~22GB ceiling while llama.cpp keeps generating (slow, but it finishes). Manual layer-offload also beats Ollama’s auto-split on time-to-first-token during spill. Different tool for “always in VRAM” vs “might spill.” Related: the USB-C-for-tools framing still saves people from N custom connectors — https://youtu.be/w2jZ_D8y-R8

**Title:** Your agent isn’t forgetful — your trimmer deleted the system prompt
**Body:**
