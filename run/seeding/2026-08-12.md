### 2. dev.to — short post
**Title:** Your RAG isn’t dumb — your retriever is ranking garbage
**Body:**
Blaming the LLM for bad answers is the default. Often the retriever already handed it noise.

A cross-encoder rerank step (a few lines) re-scores candidates so the top-k are actually relevant before generation. No bigger model required.

I walked through the five-line pattern here:
https://youtu.be/NR_EI6Va550
(related tooling: one protocol instead of N custom connectors — https://youtu.be/w2jZ_D8y-R8)
